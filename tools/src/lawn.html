Fall checklist

Get the yard ready for winter.

Fall work is what sets up a Colorado Springs yard for next year. Here is what it needs from now until spring, and why.

  1. Before the first hard freeze

    Sprinkler blowout

    Compressed air clears every zone so trapped water can't freeze and crack pipes, valves and heads. Book it before the freeze, not after.

  2. September and October

    Core aeration and overseeding

    Clay packs down over a summer of foot traffic and wind. Pulling plugs now lets water and seed reach the roots while the soil is still warm.

  3. October and November

    Leaf cleanup

    Leaves left on the lawn all winter mat down and smother the grass under them. We rake, mulch-mow or haul them off, your call.

  4. February

    Lock in your spring crew

    People here lock in their spring crew in February. Fall customers get first call on spring spots, so there is nothing to chase.

  5. April

    Sprinkler startup

    We turn the water back on, check every zone and set the controller for deep, less frequent watering.

Why aeration and deep watering pay off here

A lot of yards in the Springs sit on clay-heavy soil, and the wind dries a lawn out fast. Clay packs tight, so short, frequent watering runs off or stays near the surface.

Core aeration opens the soil back up. Watering deeply and less often pushes roots down, where they ride out dry spells. We set your controller that way at spring startup, within whatever the current local watering rules allow.
