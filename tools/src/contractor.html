What we build

Five kinds of work, each quoted line by line. One lead carpenter runs your job from demo to final inspection.

  • Kitchen remodels

    Cabinets, counters, tile and lighting, plus the plumbing and electrical moves behind them. We schedule the work so your kitchen is down for as few days as possible, and you know which days ahead of time.

    In the quote: demo, cabinets, counters, tile, fixtures, electrical, permit.

  • Bathroom remodels

    Tub-to-shower conversions, tiled showers built over a real waterproofing membrane, new vanities, and exhaust fans vented outside instead of into the attic.

    In the quote: demo, waterproofing, tile, vanity, fixtures, fan, permit when plumbing moves.

  • Basement finishing

    Framing, insulation, drywall, and a bathroom or wet bar if you want one. A basement bedroom needs a code egress window, so we size it and permit it before the first stud goes up.

    In the quote: framing, egress window, insulation, electrical, drywall, flooring, permit.

  • Decks and patios

    Composite or cedar decks with the ledger board flashed and bolted to the house, footings dug below the frost line, and railings built to code. Concrete and paver patios too.

    In the quote: footings, framing, decking, railings, stairs, permit.

  • Additions and repairs

    Bump-outs, mudrooms and full room additions tied into your existing roof, plus the repairs an older house needs: rotted sills, sagging floors, siding and storm damage.

    In the quote: foundation, framing, roofing, siding, interior finish, permit.

Building in El Paso County

Permits, hail and March snow

Building here means a regional building department, a long hail season and a snowiest month that lands in spring. We plan for all three before your quote is final.

Permits go through Pikes Peak Regional

The Pikes Peak Regional Building Department issues permits and runs inspections for Colorado Springs, El Paso County, Woodland Park and several nearby towns. It also licenses the contractors allowed to pull those permits.

Remodels that move walls, plumbing or wiring, finish a basement, or add square footage need one. We pull it, book each inspection, and keep it posted on site. You never chase an inspector.

Hail

The Front Range sees hail most summers. On additions we quote Class 4 impact-rated shingles, and we steer siding and decking toward materials that take a hit.

Snow load

March is usually the snowiest month in Colorado Springs, and spring snow is wet and heavy. Roofs, decks and additions are framed to the snow loads in the regional building code.

Decks book up for spring. Plan yours this winter.

Basements, kitchens and baths are winter work: inside, heated, no weather days. Deck footings need thawed ground, so the spring calendar fills first. A deck designed and permitted over the winter gets one of the first build dates.

From framing to finish

Five steps, the same on every job, so you always know which one you are in and what comes next.

  1. Walkthrough

    We measure, look behind what we can, and listen. Free, and about an hour.

  2. Written quote

    Line items, allowances for what you still need to pick, and a start date. Changes only happen by written change order.

  3. Permit

    We file with Pikes Peak Regional and schedule the work around each inspection.

  4. Build

    One lead on site every workday, and a short update every Friday: done, next, and anything you need to decide.

  5. Final walkthrough

    We walk it together, finish the punch list, and close out the permit.
