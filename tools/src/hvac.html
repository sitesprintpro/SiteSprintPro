Built for 6,035 feet

Colorado Springs sits at 6,035 feet. The air is thinner up here, so every cubic foot that reaches a gas burner carries less oxygen than it would at sea level.

That is why gas furnaces have to be set up for altitude, following the manufacturer's instructions. We check that setup on every gas furnace we install or service, and we show you what we checked.
