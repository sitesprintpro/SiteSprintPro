Color care at 6,035 feet

Sun at 6,000 feet is hard on color.

UV is stronger up here than at sea level, and the air stays dry most of the year. Both fade tone and leave color-treated hair rough.

So every color service at Oriel ends with a take-home plan, written for your hair, to keep the shade you paid for.

Your take-home plan

Wash
How often, how warm, and which shampoo keeps your tone.
Protect
A UV and heat protectant matched to your color.
Moisture
What to change once the furnace runs all winter.
Refresh
The week to come back for a gloss, before the tone drifts.

Your first visit

No surprises in the chair. Here is how a new client appointment runs.

  1. Bring photos

  3. Price before color
