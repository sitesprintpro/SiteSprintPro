Questions owners ask

All questions

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once the balance is paid, the site, the files and the domain are yours. If you ever want to leave, you take everything with you.

Are there monthly fees?

No. The only recurring cost is your domain name, about $12 a year, paid to the registrar. A care plan is optional: $100 a quarter if you want me to handle changes.

How fast is it?

Standard sites go live 2 to 3 days after the deposit and your info. Pro builds get priority, usually 1 to 2 days.

What do you need from me?

Your services, hours, service area, your logo if you have one, and 10 to 20 photos from your phone of real work, your crew or your space. I write all the words.

I already have a website. Can you fix it?

Often, yes. A refresh of an existing site is $300 to $400 when the bones are good. If it's cheaper to rebuild, I'll tell you.
